One funny line, 87 characters: a robot vacuum's deadpan excuse after eating a sock — it pleads not guilty on the grounds that the sock was on the floor and vacuuming is literally its job.

The joke is the vacuum's own legal defense, delivered completely straight: it reframes an accident as a workplace duty, so the punchline lands as a job description rather than an apology. Nothing in it is borrowed — no "it was delicious", no sock-puppet puns — and it reads aloud the way a very literal machine would defend itself, one short sentence at a time.
